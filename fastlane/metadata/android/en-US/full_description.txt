BLOCkTEL automatically blocks telemarketing calls in France.

The app uses the official list of phone prefixes assigned by ARCEP (French telecom authority) to commercial telemarketing to silently filter unwanted calls: no ring, no notification.

BLOCKED PREFIXES:
- 01 62, 01 63 (Ile-de-France / Paris area)
- 02 70, 02 71 (North-West)
- 03 77, 03 78 (North-East)
- 04 24, 04 25 (South-East)
- 05 68, 05 69 (South-West)
- 09 48, 09 49 (National VoIP)
- 09 475 to 09 479 (Overseas territories)

FEATURES:
- 17 pre-configured ARCEP prefixes
- Individual toggle per prefix
- Custom prefix support
- Blocked call counter (total + today)
- Recent blocked calls history

PRIVACY:
- No data collection
- No remote servers
- Everything stays on your device
- No advertising

Blocked calls remain visible in the system call log for full transparency.

Requires Android 10 or higher.
